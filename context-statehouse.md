# Who this is for

Rowan Flynn at Wausau Pilot & Review (WPR), a nonprofit local newsroom in Wausau, Wisconsin, covering Marathon County. This weekly brief reads what's new in the Wisconsin Legislature — introduced bills, committee hearings, floor action, and proposed administrative rules — and keeps only what matters to WPR as a newsroom or to its readers. Out of session (roughly June through December of even years) most weeks bring only a few proposed rules; that's expected.

# What matters

1. **The business and law of local news** — public notice and legal-notice requirements (a revenue line for newspapers), open records and open meetings law, reporter's privilege, public-access and records fees, anything affecting nonprofit news or local media.
2. **WPR's beats and trackers** — elections administration; PFAS and drinking water; contamination cleanup (DNR remediation); housing and evictions; assisted living and nursing home oversight (DQA); courts and criminal justice data; schools and DPI accountability; property tax, assessments, and TIF; local government budgets and shared revenue; data centers; surveillance cameras and license plate readers; opioid settlement spending; broadband; public health reporting.
3. **Anything naming Marathon County, Wausau, or nearby communities**, or shifting money or authority to or from counties and municipalities in ways Marathon County would feel.

Skip hunting and fishing seasons, professional licensing, and narrow technical code changes unless they touch a beat above.

# How to pitch

For proposed rules, lead with the agency, what the rule does, and the public comment deadline — readers can still weigh in, which makes it a story. For bills, say what it would do and where it is (introduced, hearing scheduled, passed a house). Next steps are concrete: the story angle, the local official or agency to call, or the date to put on the calendar.

# Who this digest is for

Rowan Flynn and the editor at Wausau Pilot & Review (WPR), a nonprofit local newsroom in Wausau, Wisconsin, and the Old English Collective (OEC), its 501(c)(3) building shared newsroom infrastructure. This is a monthly roundup of funding a small nonprofit newsroom can actually apply for right now: grants, fellowships, accelerators, and funded training. Written for the person who will actually write the application.

# How to rank and pitch

Only include opportunities that are currently open (or open within the coming month) with a stated deadline or rolling basis — fetch the program page to confirm status, deadline, and award amount before including it. Rank by deadline urgency first, then by fit. Put the deadline and award amount in the access field (e.g. "deadline Oct 15 · up to $25,000"). The pitch should say why WPR or OEC specifically is a plausible candidate — size, nonprofit status, civic-data focus, AI experience — not generic encouragement. Applications should name the concrete angle: which WPR/OEC work to center the application on.

# Worth surfacing

- Grants and funds for local, nonprofit, or rural news: Press Forward (national and local chapters), Knight Foundation, Lenfest, INN, LION sustainability programs, American Journalism Project, Google News Initiative, Meta/other platform funds while they exist
- Fellowships and accelerators an individual or a two-person newsroom can realistically do: RJI, ONA, Reynolds, JSK, Nieman (only where remote/short format fits a working newsroom)
- AI-specific journalism funding: any program funding AI experimentation, tooling, or infrastructure in newsrooms — WPR's strongest differentiator
- Civic-data and public-interest-tech funding that fits the Ledger Framework and Gavel: Democracy Fund, Code for America adjacent programs, university partnerships
- Funded training and cohort programs with stipends
- State and regional opportunities: Wisconsin, Midwest, or Great Lakes-specific funds

# Not worth surfacing

- Closed programs, past deadlines, or "check back next year" pages
- Awards and prizes that only confer recognition (no money)
- Programs restricted to large newsrooms, for-profits only, or countries outside the US
- Anything already listed under "already covered" unless its deadline is within 30 days — deadline reminders for previously-covered open programs are allowed and welcome; mark them "(reminder)"
